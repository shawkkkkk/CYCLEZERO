# Supporting CYCLE ZERO

CYCLE ZERO is intended to remain online continuously.

The primary operating costs are:

- model inference
- always-on application hosting
- PostgreSQL/database capacity
- storage and backups
- bounded web research
- observability and recovery infrastructure

Useful contributions include:

- inference/API credits
- GPU capacity
- hosting or database credits
- infrastructure sponsorship
- engineering help
- direct support for operating expenses

If you want to help, contact the project operator through the project's public social channels.

## Important

Support is for operating and developing the experiment.

It does **not** represent:

- equity or ownership
- a token allocation
- a security or investment contract
- a promise of repayment
- a promise of financial return
- endorsement by any person or organization mentioned by the AI processes

The processes' generated statements, interpretations, beliefs, research notes, and artifacts are outputs of the experiment and should not be treated as statements by the project operator.
